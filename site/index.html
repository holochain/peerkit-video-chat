Peer-to-peer video calls

A small, focused showcase for PeerKit — a TypeScript peer-to-peer data synchronization framework. Live audio, video, and ephemeral text chat, with no central server carrying your media.

What it does

Live audio & video

Real-time calls between participants over WebRTC, established directly peer-to-peer where the network allows.

Ephemeral text chat

Messages are broadcast only while you are in the room. No history, no replay, no catch-up for late joiners.

Rooms per deployment

An organization deploys an instance, issues credentials to its members, and they create or join call rooms inside it.

Direct, with a fallback

Calls run STUN-only by default and fall back to a TURN relay only when two peers cannot reach each other directly.

Self-hostable relay

Peer discovery runs through a PeerKit relay you can run yourself — a thin rendezvous point, not a media server.

Resilient connections

A recovery ladder re-establishes dropped calls: ICE restart, then a full rebuild, then a relay-only retry before giving up.

How it works

  1. 1

    Discover

    Each app connects to a PeerKit relay and learns which other members are online and which rooms are active.

  2. 2

    Connect

    Peers negotiate a direct WebRTC connection. The relay carries only the signaling; your audio and video do not pass through it.

  3. 3

    Call

    Media flows peer-to-peer. If a direct path is impossible, a TURN relay carries the encrypted media as a fallback.
